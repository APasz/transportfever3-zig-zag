Zigzag Station

A compact, platform-free railway station for reversing trains and building switchback or zigzag lines.

Uses

  • Create reversing points where a train arrives from one direction and departs in another.
  • Build mountain switchbacks where trains alternate direction while climbing or descending.
  • Add compact operational stops without constructing a passenger or cargo platform.

Features

  • One to eight parallel station tracks with connectable nodes at both ends.
  • One physical stopping point at the centre of each track.
  • Adjustable lengths of 5, 10, and 20 metres, then 40-metre steps through 440 metres.
  • Adjustable track spacing from 3 to 20 metres, in one-metre steps.
  • Optional mod setting to disable both construction and maintenance costs.
  • Independent passenger and cargo loading controls.
  • Passenger capacity adjustable from 0.5× to 3×, with 1× as the default.
  • Catchment radius adjustable from 50 to 300 metres.
  • Vanilla track type, high-speed track, and catenary options during construction.
  • Compatible with passenger and cargo consists in the line manager.
  • Compatible track mods can be applied afterwards with the normal track-upgrade tool.
  • Track upgrades apply to every track in a Zigzag Station together.
  • Supports catenary, third-rail, and other always-electric track types.
  • Localised construction text for every language shipped with Transport Fever 3.

Important

This is a track-only station with no visible platform. Loading is disabled by default, preserving its role as a compact reversing stop. Passenger and cargo loading can be enabled independently during construction. Passenger loading adds invisible pedestrian access; either loading mode adds catchment coverage at both ends and the midpoint.

The station does not explicitly command a train to reverse. Transport Fever's route solver reverses the train when the next leg of its line requires departure in the opposite direction.
