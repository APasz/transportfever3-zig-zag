Zigzag Station

A compact, platform-free rail station for reversals, switchbacks, and turnbacks.

Features

  • One to eight tracks, 5–440 metres long, with 3–20 metre spacing.
  • Independent passenger and cargo loading without visible platforms.
  • Adjustable passenger capacity and 50–300 metre catchment radius.
  • Vanilla track and catenary selection; compatible with track upgrades.
  • Four construction and maintenance cost modes.

Cost modes

  • No Cost: free construction and maintenance.
  • Track Cost: the selected track's per-metre cost for every track.
  • Module Cost: the equivalent vanilla track-module cost, scaled by length (default).
  • Station Cost: Module Cost plus vanilla passenger and cargo platform modules for the enabled loading modes.

Passenger and cargo loading are off by default. The station does not force a reversal. Trains reverse when the next leg of their route requires it. Existing Off/On cost settings migrate to No Cost/Track Cost.
